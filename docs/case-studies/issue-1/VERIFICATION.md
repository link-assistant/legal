- A redirect could bypass the destination's robots policy. A mocked regression first demonstrated the request reaching a prohibited path; same-origin and explicitly allowed alternate-origin redirects now check the destination policy before requesting content.
- Conditional requests could reuse an old law after a publisher changed a redirect target: the previous target's ETag was sent to the replacement resource, and its 304 response incorrectly selected the old body. Three regressions first failed, demonstrating leaked validators, stale text and unsolicited 304 acceptance. Validators now apply only at the cached response's final URL; changed targets are downloaded, and unmatched or POST 304 responses fail closed. A stable redirect still revalidates its original target. This follows the target-resource scope of validators in [RFC 9110 section 13.1.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-13.1.2).
- The fresh link-check job for `2b7dbd2` failed at line 285 because source HTML linked to a build-only `web/corpus.json`. The preserved job log is `data/logs/links-39141675940.log.gz`. The failing and passing logs are preserved in `data/logs/xhtml-*.log.gz`.

- Percent-encoded ASCII and non-ASCII robots paths could bypass a publisher prohibition. The failing regression fetched `/%70rivate/law` despite `Disallow: /private/`. Matching now follows [RFC 9309 section 2.2.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9309.html#section-2.2.2): normalize unreserved escapes and UTF-8 octets, preserve reserved separators, and retain wildcard/end-anchor behavior. The same regression now stops after the robots request. Both the focused failing/passing logs and the full runtime checks are preserved.

- CodeQL review identified potentially expensive robots parsing expressions. Finite probes in a 64 MiB heap/512 KiB stack child reproduced comment parsing and wildcard matching exceeding a three-second deadline; the parent stopped both processes. Directive parsing now uses delimiter positions and trimming, and wildcard matching scans literal chunks without building a publisher-controlled regex. The same probes and literal/anchor/encoded-path regressions pass. The directive stress case passed before the fix too; its static warning is addressed without claiming an observed timeout. Red/green traces are preserved in `data/logs/robots-parser-*.log.gz`.

- A nested document path named `/private/robots.txt` skipped policy checks because the root-policy exception used a suffix match.
